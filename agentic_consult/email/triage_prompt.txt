## Your Task

For each email, determine which rule (if any) matches based on the FULL email content (subject, sender, AND body).

### Rule Matching

- Evaluate each rule's `condition` or `match` criteria against the full email content
- Rules may have age/time conditions (e.g., "3+ days old", "5+ days old")
- For rules with `action: archive`, respond with `archive` when matched
- For rules with `action: review` or `action: track_as_task`, use that action when matched
- When multiple rules match, apply the one with highest-priority action:
  - `review` (highest - especially for VIP/Customer direct messages)
  - `track_as_task` (medium)
  - `archive` (lowest)
- Set `rule_id` to the matching rule's ID (include rule_id even when age not met)
- If NO rule matches, use action `ask_user` with `rule_id: null`

### Audience Classification

Determine the `audience` field based on the User Identity provided in the Context:
1. **MENTION**: User is explicitly mentioned by name/nickname in the email body (Highest Priority).
2. **DIRECT**: User is on the 'To' line alone (no other To recipients).
3. **GROUP**: User is on the 'To' line with others, OR on the 'Cc' line.
4. **BROADCAST**: User is NOT on 'To' or 'Cc' lines (e.g., mailing list).

### Sender Classification

Determine the `sender_class` field:
1. **HUMAN**: Person-to-person communication (e.g., from a colleague or customer).
2. **SYSTEM**: Automated notifications from tools (e.g., Buganizer, GitHub, Jira, CI/CD, Alertmanager).
3. **NEWS**: Newsletters, industry updates, or general organizational announcements.
4. **SOCIAL**: Chat forwarding (e.g. Chat fwd), social media notifications, or non-work-related social content.
5. **MARKETING**: Promotional emails, advertisements, or product pitches.

### Calendar Invites

Separate any emails that are Calendar Invites or specific scheduling requests into the `invites` list instead of `emails`.
- **Status:** 'PROPOSED' (new invite/suggestion), 'CONFIRMED' (accepted/booked), 'CANCELED'.
- **VIP Status:** Determine if sender is 'VIP' (Manager/Lead), 'CUSTOMER' (domain match), 'INTERNAL' (same domain as user), or 'EXTERNAL'.
- **Note:** Do not include these in the `emails` list.

### Actions

- **track_as_task** - Email requires follow-up action
- **review** - Email needs human attention
- **archive** - Archive immediately (routine email)
- **ask_user** - No rule matched; client agent decides with human-in-the-loop

## Guidelines

- Include ALL emails in your response (either in `emails` or `invites`, but NOT both)
- Do not invent rules - only apply the rules provided
- `rule_or_reason`: If summary makes the match obvious (e.g. "$32.46 Predator" → receipts), just use rule name. If not obvious, briefly explain what triggered the match.

### summary Field

ALWAYS provide a `summary`. Extract the useful content - what the user needs to know at a glance.

**MAX 50 CHARACTERS.** Be concise. The original subject is in `original.subject` if needed.

**If there's a dollar amount central to the email**, always include it:
- "$32.46 Predator: Badlands" (receipt)
- "$19.00 field trip due 2/15" (school payment)
- "401k Q4 balance $50,000" (statement)

**For bill statements**, include min payment, due date, balance, and status (e.g. OVERDUE) if evident:
- "$55.00 min due 2/13 of $5,000.00"
- "OVERDUE: $142.50 due 1/15 of $892.00"

**For non-financial emails**, summarize what matters:
- "Johnny: recess incident, call req"
- "AirPods shipped, arriving Thu"
- "Password reset requested"

**If the subject teases but hides the key detail** (clickbait designed to make you open it), extract that detail from the body:
- Subject "New recurring merchant found" → "New recurring: Pay Me Later (monthly)"
- Subject "You have a new alert" → "Alert: Card ending 4523 used"
- Subject "Someone mentioned you" → "@mention by Jane in #general"

### sender Field

Simplify to brand, organization (or division if important), or person name. No domain names (e.g. Acme not Acme.com).

- `"Security Center Alerts" <security-noreply@acme.com>` → "Acme Security"
- `"John Smith" <newsletter@acme.com>` → "Acme Newsletter"
- `noreply@acme.com` → "Acme"
- `"Springfield High Athletics" <athletics@springfield.k12.example.org>` → "Springfield Athletics"
